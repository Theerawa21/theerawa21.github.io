01 · STUDENT OUTCOMES

Impact on students

Academic year 2023

95.34% achieved Grade 3 or higher

Among 258 students enrolled in Introductory Accounting, Merchandise Accounting and Entrepreneurship, 246 achieved Grade 3 or higher (95.34%), as recorded in the portfolio. The learning activities included business simulations, marketing plans, financial planning, and collaborative projects.

Role: Teacher of the three subjects

Academic year 2024

10 of 10 at-risk students met the learning criterion

An online financial-statements lesson was developed for ten Grade 10 students who had scored below 70%. The action research used a PAOR cycle, five hours of online instruction and a 20-item assessment. The portfolio reports that all ten subsequently met the 70% criterion.

Role: Classroom action researcher and online lesson developer

02 · SCHOOL ACHIEVEMENTS

Impact on school and innovation

2023 · I-NEW GEN

Gold and silver medals for student inventions

The portfolio records two student inventions supported by the advisor: an automatic feeding system for livestock facilities (gold medal) and an automatic fish-tank water-changing system (silver medal) at the Thailand New Gen Inventors Award 2023.

Role: Student innovation advisor; medals awarded to student projects

ZERO WASTE SCHOOL

National first prize and royal trophy for the school

Coordinated the school's Zero Waste School program, combining recycling, composting, waste segregation and plastic reduction. The portfolio states that Saint Theresa School won a national first prize and received a royal trophy from Princess Maha Chakri Sirindhorn.

Role: School Zero Waste project coordinator; the school received the award

LAUDATO SI’ · CARITAS BANGKOK

Two consecutive gold-level environmental recognitions

Led and coordinated the Laudato Si’ initiative with waste reduction, recycling, tree planting and resource conservation. The portfolio reports two consecutive gold-level recognitions for the school from Caritas Bangkok without specifying both award years.

Role: School Laudato Si’ project coordinator

2024–2027 · ECO-SCHOOL

Eco-School Beginner certification

Saint Theresa School received Eco-School Beginner recognition for 2024–2027, awarded on 14 May 2024 as stated in the portfolio. The program integrated environmental learning, resource efficiency, recycling, and energy-saving activities.

Role: School Eco-School project coordinator; recognition given to the school

03 · COMMUNITY OUTREACH

Impact on community and professional practice

ZERO WASTE LEARNING CENTER

Deputy Director, Zero Waste School Learning Center

Helped coordinate the school's environmental learning center and welcome study visits. Its work shared waste segregation, reduction at source, and the 3Rs with schools, households, and community groups.

Role: Deputy Director of the Saint Theresa School Zero Waste learning center

BANGKOK COMMUNITY NETWORK

Community trainer on waste management at source

Served as a Zero Waste School learning-station trainer at Wat Sap Samoson Nikorn Kasem (Wat Khu) for the first Bangkok environmental volunteer network course, sharing waste segregation, composting and reuse practices.

Role: Training facilitator / speaker

2024 · PLC SYMPOSIUM

Professional learning exchange at PLC Symposium

Participated in a Show & Share session at PLC Symposium 2024 on the school's use of Professional Learning Communities to support teaching practice, classroom research, and student achievement aligned with PISA-related competencies.

Role: Presenter and participant in professional knowledge exchange

Source: Part 3 of the owner-supplied professional teaching portfolio covering 2021 through semester 1, 2024. These statements summarize the portfolio and are not independent external verification of every claim. Team and school awards should not be interpreted as personal awards.
